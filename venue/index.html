Venue

VENUE

The 16th Nordic Baltic Femtochemistry Conference will take place in the lecture hall of the Veröld - House of Vigdís bulding at University of Iceland.

The address is Brynjólfsgata 1, 107 Reykjavík.

HOW TO ARRIVE

Keflavík International Airport is located about 55 km or 45 minutes drive from Reykjavík. Airport Direct and Flybus operate transfers from the airport to Reykjavík in connection with all arriving flights. The Flybus runs to the the BSÍ bus terminal in Reykjavík, which is located a 15–20 minute walk from the university campus. Both companies also offer hotel drop-offs. Tickets can be purchased online in advance or at kiosks in the arrivals hall. Taxi service is also available at the airport.

Within the city, you can use public transport, or rent an electric scooter.
