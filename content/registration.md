Early-bird registration closes on **May 1, 2026**.

To register for the conference, please fill in the [registration form](/registration-form/).

### Registration fees (EUR)

|                     | Early bird (until May 1, 2026) | After May 1, 2026 |
|---------------------|--------------------------------| ----------------- |
| Regular             | 460 €                          | 570 €             |
| Student*            | 310 €                          | 410 €             |
| Accompanying person | 220 €                          | 220 €             |

*Students are required to attach a copy of their university enrollment certificate or alternatively
a copy of their student card during registration.

---

### The registration fee includes:

#### Participants (regular and students)
* Access to all scientific sessions
* Lunches at the university canteen during the conference
* Coffee breaks
* [Conference dinner](/venue/#conference-dinner) (Tuesday, June 30)

#### Accompanying persons
* Lunches at the university canteen during the conference
* [Conference dinner](/venue/#conference-dinner) (Tuesday, June 30)

For questions regarding the registration, please contact the organizers at [nbfc2026@gmail.com](mailto:nbfc2026@gmail.com).
