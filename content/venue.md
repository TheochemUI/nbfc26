## HOW TO ARRIVE

[Keflavík International Airport](https://www.kefairport.com/) is located about 55 km or 45 minutes drive from Reykjavík. 
[Airport Direct](https://airportdirect.is/) and [Flybus](https://www.re.is/tour/flybus/) operate transfers from the 
airport to Reykjavík in connection with all arriving flights. The [Flybus](https://www.re.is/tour/flybus/) runs to the 
the BSÍ bus terminal in Reykjavík, which is located a 15–20 minute walk from the university campus. Both companies also 
offer hotel drop-offs. Tickets can be purchased online in advance or at kiosks in the arrivals hall. 
[Taxi service](https://www.kefairport.com/taxi) is also available at the airport.

Within the city, you can use [public transport](https://www.straeto.is/en), or rent an
[electric scooter](https://hopp.bike/).
