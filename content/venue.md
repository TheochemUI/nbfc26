## HOW TO ARRIVE

[Keflavík International Airport](https://www.kefairport.com/) is located about 55 km or 45 minutes drive from Reykjavík. 
The [Flybus](https://www.re.is/tour/flybus/) operates between Keflavík Airport and the BSÍ bus terminal in Reykjavík in 
connection with all arriving flights. The service also offers hotel drop-offs in Reykjavík. Tickets can be purchased 
online in advance or at kiosks in the arrivals hall. [Taxi service](https://www.kefairport.com/taxi) is also available 
at the airport.

Once in the city, you can use [public transport](https://www.straeto.is/en), or rent an 
[electric scooter](https://hopp.bike/).
