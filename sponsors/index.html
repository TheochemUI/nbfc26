Information for Sponsors

The 16th Nordic Baltic Femtochemistry Conference (NBFC) is scheduled to take place from June 29 to July 1, 2026 in Reykjavík, Iceland.

The Nordic Baltic Femtochemistry Conference has established itself as a recognized platform for the ultrafast science community, typically attracting around 100 participants from the Nordic and Baltic countries, as well as from elsewhere. NBFC 2026, hosted for the first time at the University of Iceland in Reykjavík, will highlight the latest developments in ultrafast chemistry, physics, and materials science, as well as advancements in optical and X-ray techniques and for experiments with atto- and femtosecond resolution.

We invite reputable companies in the ultrafast laser and optics industry, whose expertise aligns with the theme of the conference, to use the possibility to join NBFC 2026 as a sponsor. Your participation as an industrial partner not only allows you to engage with around a hundred leading researchers and professionals, but also offers a unique opportunity to showcase your current product portfolio to a diverse and knowledgeable audience.

We are confident that your presence will be highly beneficial, offering you a platform to network with potential clients and partners actively involved in the field of ultrafast science. We encourage you to consider introducing your latest product portfolio during the conference at a conference booth. This will enable you to spotlight your innovations and engage directly with researchers who share a profound interest in femtosecond spectroscopy and other time-resolved techniques.

If your company is keen on seizing this opportunity and contributing to the success of NBFC 2026, we kindly request you to get in touch with us at your earliest convenience. We look forward to the possibility of your company joining us as a sponsor at NBFC 2026. Your participation will enrich the overall experience for all attendees and contribute to advancements in our field.

Thank you for considering our invitation. We look forward to collaborating with you!
